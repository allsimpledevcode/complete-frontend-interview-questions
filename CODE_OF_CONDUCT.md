# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We want this repository to be a safe, welcoming, and helpful place for everyone interested in frontend interview preparation.  
We pledge to respect all contributors, regardless of background, experience level, or opinion.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior:
- Sharing accurate and relevant frontend interview questions.
- Providing constructive and respectful feedback.
- Being open to learning from others.
- Giving credit where due.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sharing misleading or incorrect information intentionally.
- Plagiarizing content without proper attribution.
- Posting spam, promotional links, or irrelevant material.

## Our Responsibilities
Repo maintainers are responsible for:
- Clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior.
- Reviewing, editing, or removing contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
- Banning users for repeated or severe violations.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to:
- The repository and its discussions.
- Pull requests, issues, and comments.
- Any related community spaces.

## Enforcement
If you see a violation:
- Report it by opening a confidential issue or contacting the repository owner directly.
- All reports will be reviewed and investigated.
- Maintainers will take actions they deem necessary, which may include warnings, content removal, or banning.
